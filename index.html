Our Roots & Community

MORE THAN CHAI.

“এক কাপ চা আর অনন্ত আড্ডা”

(A cup of tea and endless conversations)

CHA KHOR is a casual place for good tea, quick bites, long conversations and even longer evenings. Rooted in the timeless adda tradition of Krishnanagar, Nadia—celebrated for its clay-pot artisans, literature, and passionate college debates—we built a room where time simply slows down.

Whether you are slipping away between college lectures, gathering with childhood friends after months apart, or finding a quiet corner to read with fresh milk tea, CHA KHOR belongs to you. Come for the chai. Stay for the vibe.
